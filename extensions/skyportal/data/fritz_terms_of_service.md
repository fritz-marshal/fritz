Fritz is a managed SkyPortal deployment operated by the University of Minnesota, the University of California, Berkeley, the California Institute of Technology, and the Université Paris-Saclay. It is provided on a best-effort basis for the research use of the collaborations it serves, with no guarantee of uptime and no warranty. The level of service depends on continued project funding, and may be reduced to maintenance only. Please do not treat it as the only copy of anything you care about.

By using Skyportal/Fritz you agree to the following:

**Your account is yours.** Do not share your credentials or API tokens, and please use the API at a reasonable rate. If you need a higher rate for a short period, contact the Skyportal/Fritz team.

**Keep it professional.** Comments and annotations should stay respectful and professional. Treat restricted data as restricted, including screenshots and exports, and do not share them outside the group that holds the data.

**Respect data access.** Data you can see through a team/group you belong to, e.g. photometry, spectroscopy, analyses and comments, stays within that team/group unless the people who provided it agree otherwise. The same goes for anything you can see because someone shared it with you directly. Where data carries its own access terms, e.g. ZTF partnership data, those terms apply here as well.

**Share back.** If you take follow-up on an object in Skyportal/Fritz, register your telescope in the database following the instructions and record that you have the data, even if the data itself stays restricted to your group. Anyone who can see the object will see that follow-up exists and who to contact, but not the data itself, so that people know who to ask before duplicating it. If you need access granted, contact the Skyportal/Fritz team. Candidates surfaced through a group's shared filters should be posted back to that group.

**Cite what you use.** Peer reviewed articles, AstroNotes, TNS reports and public presentations coming out of work done here should cite SkyPortal (van der Walt et al. 2019, Coughlin et al. 2023) and the alert broker (e.g. BABAMUL, BOOM, Fink, ...) that supplied the data, along with the specific broker-filter, and should acknowledge the facilities and programs that provided the follow-up. A standard acknowledgement block you can copy is linked from the Source pages.

**Help us keep it working.** Report bugs preferentially via git-issue on https://github.com/skyportal, and answer the occasional question or survey from the Skyportal team. This is mostly how we find out what to fix.

Authorship and publication norms are set by your collaboration rather than by these terms, e.g. the ZTF partnership publication policy.

Activity on Fritz is logged for operational and debugging purposes. We may update these terms, and will announce anything substantive.
