Fritz is a managed SkyPortal deployment operated by the University of Minnesota, the University of California, Berkeley, the California Institute of Technology, and Université Paris-Saclay. It is provided on a best-effort basis for the research use of the collaborations it serves, with no guarantee of uptime and no warranty. Please do not treat it as the only copy of anything you care about.

By using Fritz you agree to the following:

**Your account is yours**. Do not share your credentials or API tokens, and please use the API at a reasonable rate.

**Respect data access.** Data you can see through a group you belong to stays within that group unless the people who provided it agree otherwise. The same goes for anything you can see because someone shared it with you directly. Where data carries its own access terms, e.g. ZTF partnership data, those terms apply here as well.

**Share back.** If you take follow-up on an object in Fritz, register in Fritz that you have it, even if the data itself stays restricted to your group. Other folks should at least know who to ask. Candidates surfaced through a group's shared filters should be posted back to that group.

**Cite what you use.** Papers, AstroNotes and TNS reports coming out of work done here should cite SkyPortal and the alert broker that supplied the data, along with the specific filter, and should acknowledge the facilities and programs that provided the follow-up. We maintain a standard block you can copy.

**Help us keep it working.** Report bugs, and answer the occasional question or survey from the team. This is mostly how we find out what to fix.

Authorship and publication norms are set by your collaboration rather than by these terms, e.g. the ZTF partnership publication policy.

Activity on Fritz is logged for operational and debugging purposes. We may update these terms, and will announce anything substantive.
